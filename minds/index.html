Research topic

Minds and meaning

The questions underneath the rest: how sound turns into meaning, whether a system could reason in steps we can read, why we tie evil to the dark, and who counts as a mind at all. Most of this is essays. The experimental side is small: a story-writing model whose inner workings I probed to watch meaning build up, word by word.
