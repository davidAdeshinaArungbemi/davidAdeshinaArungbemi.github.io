Publications

  1. Preprint · not peer-reviewed · 2026

    Narrative Transportation and Speciesism in Large Language Models

    D. A. Arungbemi. Zenodo, 2026.

    Can a story, given in context, shift a language model toward caring about animals? I gave models a story about an animal, the same facts written plainly, or an essay arguing for animals. Only the story clearly shifted their answers toward caring about that animal.

    doi:10.5281/zenodo.21294610PDF

  2. Journal article · peer-reviewed · 2025

    An End to End Wearable Device and System for Indefinite, Continuous, Real Time Gesture Recognition of Directional and Shape-Based Arm Gestures

    Co-author. IEEE Access 13, 153436–153463, 2025.

    A wearable that recognises arm gestures, both directions and drawn shapes, continuously and in real time. I designed its AI, from the raw motion data to the model that names each gesture, and the interface of the live app: how people experience the system as they use it.
