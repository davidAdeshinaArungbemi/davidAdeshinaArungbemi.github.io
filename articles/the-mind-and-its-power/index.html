26 Oct 2025 — The Mind and Its Power

Suzume · David Adeshina Arungbemi

When I browse Pinterest, I see a story not just art. I guess some possibility of what could be.
Each art, design, visualisation, photograph, has some meaning beyond what we normally observe.
They draw me in,
They’re beautiful.

A world, a culture, a reality, a person, entity: their actions, interactions, the rules and their manipulations and their constraints, their creativity.

I don’t want to draw or spend time sketching, I don’t want to spend hours building something. For I will surely be disappointed when my hand creations do not match my expectations. These take time, I cannot submit to time.

But make decisions, come up with ideas and tell the system to give me what I want. A method that is intuitive to man, one without words or showing.
I want to be a creator, I want to free man from his physical limitations and allow him to make full use of his creativity to the utmost. There is power in the human mind, but day to day problems, the greed of man and his short-sightedness limits it. He oppresses himself, he oppresses his fellow man.

Autonomy to each person, each person a “god” but not the God.

Each human being is special. It’s just that society doesn’t allow him.
Each person may indeed (I cannot say) have a greater “glory” than the other but that does not mean his destiny, his “glory” is of less. He should not surrender to another.

He must be free, all must be free. I will make it so.
Man will not be restricted but the physical realm, it’s a limitation. Man is too satisfied with where he is even when he suffers. He must be pushed out for there is something better.

The Mind!

It is through The Mind, that I appreciate art or visuals. It is through The Mind that I make sense of the world around me. It is through The Mind that I feel, that I hate, that I love.

The world is pointless. Even if its existence remains, if there is no observer, if there is no Mind to make sense of it. What is the world without our perception? I say None.

We are the world!
The man is the world.

But all power belongs to The True Creator.

I may not be The True Creator, I may be a Sub-Creator, it matters not.

But how?
I lament.

The physical world will not allow me.
Her limitations, her constraints do not make it easy.
The only solution is time.
But time waits for no man!
How can i bring it to man now!

Oh I wish I could explore the stars, the nebula, the planets, other galaxies.

The Mind!
The Mind!
The Mind
The Mind
The Mind!!!!!!!!!!!!

How do I bring our dreams to us?
The Mind must hallucinate these. But what if we are already hallucinating.
