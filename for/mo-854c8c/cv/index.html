Fellowships & programmes

Research Fellow, MARS 5.0, Cambridge AI Safety Hub

2026 – now

A mentored AI safety research programme. I test whether training a language model on a story changes the values it acts on, comparing each story with the same text rewritten neutrally or with its ending flipped. I design the experiments and datasets, run the training (supervised fine-tuning, distillation, GRPO) on Gemma and Qwen models, and present results to the cohort. Mentor: Elizaveta Tennant (Google DeepMind / UCL).

  • Looking inside. I trained a small model on a fable and used a readout of what each layer is disposed to say (Anthropic’s Jacobian lens) to look for the fable inside it, though its reasoning never named it. With the fable in context the readout found it plainly; in the trained model the signal for the fable’s moral was matched by a control, so it came from the training, not the fable.
  • Describing versus using a reasoning process. I trained a small model on first-person accounts of how it reasons: by projecting a known story onto a new situation (Mark Turner, The Literary Mind). It described the process almost every time it was asked and used that form in over a third of 2,000 reasoning traces, but never retrieved a real story: whether it learned the process or only its words is still open.
  • Reinforcement learning on reading. With GRPO, a model read a story scene by scene and chose a moral emotion toward each character; on three held-out stories, the share of choices that matched the story’s own direction rose from 53% to 76% in an early test.
