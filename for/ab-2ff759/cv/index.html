Fellowships & programmes

Research Fellow, MARS 5.0, Cambridge AI Safety Hub

2026 – now

A mentored AI safety research programme. I test whether training a language model on a story changes the values it acts on, comparing each story with the same text rewritten neutrally or with its ending flipped. I design the experiments and datasets, run the training (supervised fine-tuning, distillation, GRPO) on Gemma and Qwen models, and present results to the cohort. Mentor: Elizaveta Tennant (Google DeepMind / UCL).

  • Describing versus using a reasoning process. I trained a small model on first-person accounts of how it reasons: by projecting a known story onto a new situation (Mark Turner, The Literary Mind). It described the process almost every time it was asked and brought that story into its reasoning on about 70% of 2,000 dilemmas, yet its overall decisions barely changed: whether it learned the process or only its words is still open.
  • A story that acts without being named. Trained on a fable written as its own answers about itself, a small model (Gemma 4) shifted its decisions the way the fable does when it is in the prompt, while a control trained on an unrelated text moved the other way; no reasoning trace named the fable.
