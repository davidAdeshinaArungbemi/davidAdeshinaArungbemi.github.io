Prepared for Professor Alain Trémeau · Laboratoire Hubert Curien, Université Jean Monnet

The question I would bring

Your recent work learns colour difference as people with a given visual impairment perceive it, from a small number of judgements combined with standard colour-difference formulas, and reads style and emotion in dance from movement.

Both touch things I have worked on. I tried to predict one person’s response to images from their ranked image history; I trained a small transformer on the colour palettes of Cubist paintings; and I built the AI of a wearable that recognises arm gestures from raw motion data. I would like to bring model training to your questions: personal models of colour perception, and movement read from sensors as well as from video.

Work that bears on it

A model meant to reason in colour 2026

I trained a small transformer on the palettes of over 2,000 Cubist paintings to restore a palette after its colours were scrambled. I set out to build a model that reasons in colour; it became something else, and my essay is about what.

One person’s response to images 2026

I built a model that predicts fMRI responses (BOLD5000) to new images, personalised from a person’s ranked image history, and an app that tunes generative art to raise the prediction. The predictions were weak overall and best in visual areas; my write-up analyses why.

Movement from motion sensors Gyronics · IEEE Access 2025

I designed the AI of a wearable that recognises arm gestures in real time, from raw motion data to the model that names each gesture (co-author, peer-reviewed), and built a view that draws each gesture as the path of a virtual pen.

What preferences reveal MARS · 2026

In the MARS fellowship I train language models and test what changes, against matched controls. In my tests, a student trained only on a teacher model’s stated preferences over fruits, countries and languages moved toward the teacher’s values, though the story behind them was never named.

Restoring paintings with missing pixels 2025

I trained a model to restore Symbolist paintings (WikiArt) with 30% of their pixels missing. It keeps colour and layout, and stays blurry.
