Publications & preprints

Narrative Transportation and Speciesism in Large Language Models

2026

Sole author. Preprint, Zenodo (CC BY 4.0), doi:10.5281/zenodo.21294610. Futurekind Fellowship research track.

  • Adapted Green & Brock's narrative-transportation paradigm for LLMs: 12 animals × 5 instruction-tuned models, 2,560 trials, with matched factual and control conditions.
  • Narratives reduced expressed speciesism on animal-specific items from 0.093 to 0.016 (Wilcoxon p = .005); matched factual texts left it unchanged. Averaging with near-floor general items nearly hid the effect — a case study in how a summary score can conceal a targeted change.
  • A pro-use persuasive essay nearly tripled the odds of a speciesist answer, outperforming the matched pro-use narrative: argument beat story when the aim was loosening concern.
  • Released the full stimulus set, experiment runner and analysis pipeline; three independent replications (second story set, off-topic control, summarisation framing) confirmed the core effect.

An End to End Wearable Device and System for Indefinite, Continuous, Real Time Gesture Recognition of Directional and Shape-Based Arm Gestures

Aug 2025

The Narrative Hypothesis  ·  Part II: Complexity  ·  I Ran the Experiment. Here Is What I Found.

Apr 2026

Epistemology & multi-agent evaluation · essays + experiment. Narrative proposed as a distinct problem-solving domain, then as a tangled graph of levels (person, family, community, nation) interacting only through representatives. The empirical test — a solo analytical agent versus a creative/translator/judge hybrid across ten checkpoints (Llama 3.3 70B, Gemini Flash Lite) — returned a null result (7/10 vs 6/10); reported as such, with the design flaws named and a valid version specified in advance.

I Gave Four AI Agents a Philosophy and Put Them Under Pressure.

Mar 2026

Alignment · 640-turn multi-agent simulation (Qwen 2.5 7B). Kantian, Utilitarian, Stoic and Survival agents in a resource-sharing game under sustained scarcity. No philosophy ever fully defected, but stated values and behaviour drifted apart: the Kantian agent kept its language while its giving tracked its budget. Values held strongly under normal conditions and loosely under pressure, suggesting prompting is not the tool for instilling values that must survive cost.
